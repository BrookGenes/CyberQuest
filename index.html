🔐 Protect your accounts

Use unique passwords.
Don't reuse the same password everywhere.
Turn on MFA.
A second verification step can stop an attacker even if your password is stolen.

🎣 Beat phishing

Slow down.
Scammers often create urgency so you don't think.
Check the sender and link.
Don't click just because a message looks official.

📱 Think before posting

Information adds up.
Small details about your school, location, pets, birthday, or routines can reveal more than you expect.

🤖 Be smart with AI

Verify surprising requests.
AI can make convincing messages, images, and voices. Verify through a trusted channel.
